Poster series · 2026

A FEAST WITHOUT TOMORROW

A series of four posters inspired by the Red Wedding. Here, the celebration becomes a trap: the invitation promises safety, trust collapses, the future is cut short, and in the end only a mother remains among the extinguished candles.

A red thread runs through all four works. It binds those who came to the feast together, then snaps at the moment when nothing can be undone.
